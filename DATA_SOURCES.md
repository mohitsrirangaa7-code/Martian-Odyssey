# NASA Data Sources & Datasets

MarsWalk Explorer integrates scientific observations from multiple NASA Mars missions to create a unified, layered exploration and mission-planning environment for future human explorers.

The datasets cover surface imagery, terrain elevation, thermal properties, mineralogy, geology, atmospheric conditions, radiation, and subsurface characteristics.

## 1. Optical Imagery & Surface Mapping

These datasets provide visual representations of the Martian surface, allowing explorers to identify geological formations, craters, boulders, and potential surface hazards.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| HiRISE | Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) | High-resolution optical imagery, with resolutions reaching approximately 25–50 cm/pixel. | Identifying boulders, obstacles, and small-scale surface features. |
| CTX (Context Camera) | MRO | Regional grayscale imagery at approximately 6 m/pixel. | Regional terrain mapping and route planning. |
| THEMIS Visible | Mars Odyssey | Visible-light imagery of the Martian surface. | Geological context and regional surface mapping. |
| HRSC (High Resolution Stereo Camera) | Mars Express | Stereo optical imagery used for surface reconstruction. | Three-dimensional terrain visualization. |
| MOC (Mars Orbiter Camera) | Mars Global Surveyor | Historical wide-angle and narrow-angle surface imagery. | Geological analysis and supplementary mapping. |
| Viking Orbiter Imaging | Viking 1 & 2 | Historical optical observations of Mars. | Global and regional surface context. |

## 2. Topography & Elevation

Topographic datasets provide elevation information required to understand the physical characteristics of the Martian terrain.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOLA (Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter) | Mars Global Surveyor | Laser altimetry measurements used to create global elevation models. | Elevation mapping and regional terrain analysis. |
| HiRISE Digital Terrain Models (DTMs) | MRO | High-resolution stereo-derived elevation models. | Local slope calculations, obstacle identification, and route accessibility. |
| HRSC Digital Terrain Models | Mars Express | Stereo-derived regional elevation products. | Three-dimensional terrain reconstruction and slope analysis. |
| SHARAD | MRO | Shallow subsurface radar observations. | Investigating buried ice and subsurface geological structures. |

Derived terrain layers can include:
- Slope maps
- Elevation contours
- Terrain roughness
- Hillshade visualizations
- Aspect maps
- Three-dimensional terrain models

## 3. Thermal Properties & Surface Temperature

Thermal datasets help characterize surface temperature variations and the thermal properties of Martian geological materials.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| THEMIS Infrared | Mars Odyssey | Thermal infrared observations of the Martian surface. | Thermal mapping and identification of surface temperature variations. |
| TES (Thermal Emission Spectrometer) | Mars Global Surveyor | Thermal infrared spectroscopy providing temperature, albedo, and thermal inertia products. | Understanding surface material properties and thermal behaviour. |
| THEMIS Day/Night Mosaics | Mars Odyssey | Daytime and nighttime thermal observations. | Comparing thermal behaviour across different surface materials. |
| REMS (Rover Environmental Monitoring Station) | Curiosity | Measurements of air and ground temperature and other environmental variables. | Local environmental characterization. |
| MEDA (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer) | Perseverance | Measurements of temperature, wind, pressure, dust, and other environmental variables. | Local weather and environmental monitoring. |

## 4. Mineralogy & Spectroscopy

Mineralogical datasets help identify the chemical and mineral composition of Martian rocks and soils, enabling scientific target selection and geological interpretation.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRISM (Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars) | MRO | Visible and near-infrared spectral observations used to identify minerals. | Mapping hydrated minerals, clays, sulfates, and carbonates. |
| OMEGA | Mars Express | Visible and infrared mineralogical observations. | Regional mineralogical mapping. |
| ChemCam | Curiosity | Laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS) and remote imaging. | Analysing the elemental composition of rocks and soils. |
| SuperCam | Perseverance | Remote LIBS, Raman, infrared spectroscopy, and imaging. | Geological characterization of surface targets. |
| PIXL (Planetary Instrument for X-ray Lithochemistry) | Perseverance | X-ray fluorescence measurements of elemental composition. | Fine-scale chemical analysis of rocks. |
| SHERLOC | Perseverance | Raman and fluorescence spectroscopy. | Identifying minerals and organic compounds. |

## 5. Geological & Surface Composition

These datasets provide information about the elemental composition and geological characteristics of Mars.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| GRS (Gamma Ray Spectrometer) | Mars Odyssey | Measurements of elemental abundances, including hydrogen-related information. | Regional geological and compositional mapping. |
| DAN (Dynamic Albedo of Neutrons) | Curiosity | Neutron measurements used to investigate subsurface hydrogen. | Identifying potential water-bearing materials. |
| APXS (Alpha Particle X-ray Spectrometer) | Curiosity / Mars Exploration Rovers | Elemental composition measurements of rocks and soils. | Geological classification and chemical analysis. |
| CheMin | Curiosity | X-ray diffraction measurements of mineral composition. | Identifying minerals in collected samples. |
| RIMFAX | Perseverance | Ground-penetrating radar observations. | Investigating subsurface geological structures. |

## 6. Atmospheric Conditions & Weather

Atmospheric datasets provide information about Mars' weather, temperature profiles, dust distribution, atmospheric pressure, and seasonal environmental variations.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCS (Mars Climate Sounder) | MRO | Atmospheric infrared observations, including temperature profiles, dust, and water-ice clouds. | Atmospheric characterization and dust monitoring. |
| MAVEN | Mars Atmosphere and Volatile Evolution | Atmospheric composition, plasma, solar wind, and energetic particle measurements. | Understanding the Martian atmosphere and space environment. |
| REMS | Curiosity | Local temperature, pressure, wind, humidity, and UV measurements. | Historical surface weather observations. |
| MEDA | Perseverance | Local environmental measurements, including temperature, wind, pressure, and dust. | Local weather characterization. |

## 7. Radiation & Space Weather

Radiation datasets are particularly important for human exploration because astronauts on Mars will be exposed to galactic cosmic rays and solar energetic particles.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission / Source | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| RAD (Radiation Assessment Detector) | Curiosity | Measures radiation exposure and particle-related radiation conditions at the Martian surface. | Historical surface radiation measurements and astronaut safety context. |
| MAVEN Particle Measurements | MAVEN | Observations of energetic particles, solar wind, and plasma conditions. | Understanding the Martian space-weather environment. |
| HEND (High Energy Neutron Detector) | Mars Odyssey | Measures neutron flux. | Studying hydrogen distribution and subsurface properties. |
| CCMC Mars Space Weather Products | NASA Community Coordinated Modeling Center | Space-weather observations, analysis, and model products. | Solar activity and radiation hazard context. |

## 8. Geophysics & Subsurface Exploration

These datasets provide information about the internal structure, subsurface composition, and geological characteristics of Mars.

| Dataset / Instrument | Mission | Description | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEIS (Seismic Experiment for Interior Structure) | InSight | Seismic measurements of marsquakes and planetary activity. | Understanding Martian geophysical properties. |
| HP3 (Heat Flow and Physical Properties Package) | InSight | Subsurface thermal measurements. | Investigating heat flow and subsurface properties. |
| RISE (Rotation and Interior Structure Experiment) | InSight | Radio science observations of Mars' rotation. | Understanding the planet's interior structure. |
| MARSIS | Mars Express | Low-frequency subsurface radar observations. | Investigating buried ice and subsurface structures. |
| SHARAD | MRO | High-resolution shallow subsurface radar observations. | Mapping buried geological layers and potential ice deposits. |

## 9. Rover-Based Observations & Ground-Level Imagery

Rover datasets provide localized observations that complement orbital measurements and help validate geological interpretations.

| Rover / Mission | Available Datasets | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Perseverance (Mars 2020) | Mastcam-Z, Navcam, Hazcam, SuperCam, PIXL, SHERLOC, MEDA, RIMFAX | Surface imagery, geological analysis, environmental characterization, and scientific target identification. |
| Curiosity (Mars Science Laboratory) | Mastcam, Navcam, Hazcam, MAHLI, ChemCam, CheMin, APXS, REMS, RAD, DAN | Surface geology, radiation observations, and local environmental measurements. |
| Spirit & Opportunity (MER) | PanCam, Navcam, APXS, Mössbauer, Mini-TES, microscopic imagery | Historical surface geology and mineralogical analysis. |
| Phoenix Mars Lander | Surface imagery, robotic arm observations, meteorological and soil measurements | Polar soil and environmental characterization. |
| InSight | SEIS, HP3, RISE, meteorological observations | Geophysical and subsurface research. |

## 11. Selected Dataset Integration

The following datasets are prioritized for integration into MarsWalk Explorer:

| Layer | Selected Dataset | Primary Function |
|---|---|---|
| Optical | HiRISE / CTX | Surface imagery and obstacle identification |
| Topographic | MOLA / HiRISE DTM | Elevation, slope, and terrain analysis |
| Thermal | THEMIS / TES | Surface thermal properties |
| Mineralogical | CRISM | Mineral identification and scientific target selection |
| Atmospheric | MCS / MEDA | Atmospheric and environmental characterization |
| Radiation | RAD / MAVEN | Radiation exposure and space-weather context |
| Ground Observations | Perseverance / Curiosity | Localized scientific observations and imagery |

These datasets will be combined to provide a multi-dimensional view of selected Martian exploration regions.
